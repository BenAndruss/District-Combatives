Ben Andruss

Also known professionally as Ben Drader, and Ben Forte.

Who Is Ben Andruss?

Ben Andruss — sometimes credited as Ben Drader, or under the name Ben Forte — was the founder and Chief Instructor of District Combatives, a close-quarters training space in Washington, D.C.

Over more than 15 years, Andruss developed his approach to martial arts, self-defense, and combatives primarily through self-instruction, supplemented by formal instruction during Basic Training. He later founded District Combatives in 2016.

Training Background

  • Krav Maga
  • Jiu-Jitsu
  • Muay Thai
  • Spontaneous Protection Enabling Accelerated Response (SPEAR)
  • Special Combat Aggressive Reactionary System (SCARS)

Military & Early Career

Andruss served in the U.S. Army from 1998 to 2000 as an Ammunition Specialist, deploying to Bosnia, where he earned awards and commendations.

A Passion for Special Operations

Ben was a big fan of special operations. Despite serving in the Army (although not in the Special Forces, Ranger Battalion, or any Special Mission Unit), he was such a fan of Naval Special Operations that he got a Navy SEAL-inspired tattoo.

District Combatives

Founded in 2016, District Combatives was built around a simple idea: develop reliable combative technique that's easily recalled under duress. Not a traditional martial arts school. Not a boxing gym. A training ground.
